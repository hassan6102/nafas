Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to questions you may have about working with us

How do I track my bulk order?

You can track your order's production stages (cutting, sewing, finishing) and shipping status by logging into your client dashboard, or by reaching out directly to your assigned account manager.

What payment methods do you accept for manufacturing?

For wholesale and custom manufacturing, we accept Bank Wire Transfers (T/T) and Letters of Credit (L/C). We also accept major credit cards and PayPal for smaller sample orders.

How can I change my client portal password?

You can easily update your password by navigating to your Account Settings page after logging in, or by clicking the 'Forgot Password' link on the login screen if you are locked out.
