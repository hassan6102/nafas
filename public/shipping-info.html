Logistics FAQ

Common questions about our factory's shipping and freight operations

How can I track my bulk shipment?

Once your garments leave our factory floor, your account manager will provide you with a Bill of Lading (BOL) or an AWB tracking number to monitor your shipment in real-time.

What if our warehouse is closed upon arrival?

For LTL and FCL freight, our carriers will call your receiving department ahead of time to schedule a specific delivery appointment and ensure your loading dock is ready.

Do you offer weekend delivery?

Standard freight and container deliveries operate Monday through Friday. However, weekend delivery can be arranged for highly urgent sample air shipments upon special request.
