Have a question? Check out our FAQ

Learn more about our premium bag collections, differences between Original and Mirror quality, shipping details, and how to care for your new bag.

What is the difference between Original and Mirror quality bags?

Original bags are authentic pieces sourced directly from authorized brand distributors. Mirror quality bags are exact 1:1 replicas made with identical materials, genuine leather, and precise hardware to offer the same luxury feel at a fraction of the cost.

What does High Copy mean?

High Copy bags provide an excellent balance of style and affordability. They feature great craftsmanship and durability for everyday use, making them a practical choice if you want trendy designs without the premium price tag.

Are the product images real?

Yes, absolutely! We ensure that all product photos displayed on our website are actual images of the bags you will receive. We do not use stock photos, so you know exactly what you are purchasing.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery times depend on your location. Standard local shipping typically takes 2 to 4 business days. We will provide a tracking link as soon as your order is dispatched so you can follow its journey.

Can I return or exchange a bag if I don't like it?

Yes, customer satisfaction is our priority. You can return or exchange the bag within 14 days of receiving it, provided it is unused, in its original condition, and has all tags and packaging intact.

How should I care for my leather bags?

To keep your leather bags looking pristine, avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight and water. Clean them gently with a soft, dry cloth, and store them in the provided dust bag when not in use to maintain their shape and texture.
